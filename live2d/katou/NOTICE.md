# Katou Live2D model (third-party)

This folder contains a Live2D (Cubism 2) model that is NOT created by the site owner.

- Source: https://github.com/evrstr/live2d-widget-models (folder `live2d_evrstr/katou`)
- That collection states that the copyrights of the models belong to their respective companies or organizations and that they must not be used commercially.
- The original author of the model is not credited in the source repository.

If the rights holder or the model author asks for removal, delete this whole `live2d/katou/` folder.
The chatbot then falls back automatically to the built-in original 3D character.
